Resonant Matter Field @VERSION@

A spatial microcontact, modal, and electromechanical synthesizer. Its “grains” are microscopic force events (impacts, slips, scrapes, fractures, particulate contacts) placed inside a modeled resonant body that exchanges energy with an electromechanical transducer and is observed from two spatially separated pickups. Runtime Scala tuning maps the resonant spectrum itself, not only the MIDI notes.

On the next screen tick the formats you want (Audio Unit, VST3, standalone app). Plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins; the app goes to /Applications. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW afterwards; it appears under Resonant Matter Lab > Resonant Matter Field as an instrument.

Requires macOS 12 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
