Installed

Rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Logic: it scans on launch; Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan; Reaper: Preferences > Plug-ins > Re-scan). Look under Resonant Matter Lab > Resonant Matter Field in the instruments list.

Start with the Kinetic Plate factory sound, drag the amber contact target through the field, then load a Scala .scl file (the GitHub release zip ships 19-EDO, five-limit JI and 13-EDT Bohlen–Pierce in its Tunings folder) and compare the Nearest and Ascending resonance mappings.
