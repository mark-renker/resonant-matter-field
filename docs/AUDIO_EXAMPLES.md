# Audio examples

All examples are stereo, 48 kHz, engine-direct renders with no reverb, delay, chorus, or mastering chain. WAV files are 24-bit; M4A and MP3 copies are supplied for phone playback. The audition reel is 45.75 seconds.

1. **19-EDO Sparse Alloy** — isolated contacts, long decay, ascending 19-EDO modal lattice, wide pickups.
2. **Just Friction Cloud** — dense rough contact stream, five-limit just intonation, moving contact population.
3. **Bohlen–Pierce Constellation** — non-octave 13-EDT scale repeating at 3:1, revealing the direct resonance-lattice mapping.
4. **Active Coil Bloom** — electromechanical coupling and bounded positive active impedance drive prolonged reinforcement.

The reel presents those four scenes in order with short silences between them. Peak limiting is part of RMF's output-safety stage; no post-processing was added.
